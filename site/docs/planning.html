Planning your day

The Day view is split into a Plan lane and an Actual lane. You plan in one and record reality in the other.

Creating and editing blocks

  • Create: drag in an empty part of a lane, or double-click to create a block of the default length.
  • Move: drag a block. In the Week view you can drag blocks across days.
  • Resize: drag the top or bottom edge of a block.
  • Copy: hold Ctrl (⌘ on macOS) while dragging.
  • Fine steps: hold Shift while dragging to snap to 5 minutes instead of the default step.
  • Cancel: press Esc during a drag.

Select a block and press Enter to open the editor. There you can set the title, goal, start and end time, and notes. The time fields offer a list of slots, and moving the start time keeps the block's duration.

With a block selected, ↑ and ↓ nudge it, Shift+↑ and Shift+↓ resize it, and 1–9 set its goal. Delete removes it.

Blocks that overlap in the Plan lane are shown side by side.

Ghosts

Every planned block appears as a dashed ghost in the Actual lane until you deal with it. A ghost is a reminder, not a record. It does not count as actual time.

ActionHowResult
Happened as plannedClick ✓ or press aThe ghost becomes a confirmed actual block.
Didn't happenClick ✗, press s or DeleteThe plan block is marked as skipped.
Happened differentlyDrag or resize the ghost, or press EnterAn actual block is logged at the new time, linked to the plan.

If you change the plan before confirming, its ghost follows automatically. A skipped plan block can be brought back with Restore from its context menu.

Confirm rest of day

When the day went mostly to plan, click Confirm rest above the Actual lane or press Shift+A. Every past ghost is logged as actual time, fitted around anything you have already logged. Time after the current moment is never confirmed.

Unplanned time

Drag directly in the Actual lane to record something you didn't plan. The Actual lane never overlaps: a new actual block trims or splits the confirmed blocks it covers. You can't log time in the future.

The summary bar

The bar under the date shows totals for the day:

  • Plan: total planned time.
  • Actual: confirmed time only.
  • Unplanned: actual time that wasn't in the plan.
  • Skipped: planned time marked as not happened.
  • Unconfirmed: planned time still shown as ghosts.

Next to the totals, a chip for each goal shows actual time over planned time for that day.

Past plans

A past day's plan is locked so that your record of what you intended stays intact. To change it, click the lock icon next to Plan in the lane header. The Actual lane of past days stays editable.

Context menus and copying

Right-click a block or an empty slot for more actions:

  • On an empty slot: New block, Confirm rest of day as planned, Copy yesterday's plan and Copy last week's plan for the same weekday.
  • On a plan block: Edit, Set goal, Happened as planned, Didn't happen and Copy to tomorrow.
  • On a ghost: Happened as planned, Didn't happen and Log differently.

Undo

Every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z and redone with Ctrl+Shift+Z, or with the arrows in the top-right corner.
