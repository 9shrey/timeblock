Settings

Open Settings from the bottom of the sidebar. Changes are saved immediately.

Preferences

SettingDescription
Week starts onMonday, Sunday or Saturday. Changing it moves targets, outcomes and reflections to the matching weeks and clears the undo history.
Day starts atWhere the Day and Week views scroll to for days other than today.
Snap toThe step used when dragging blocks. Hold Shift for 5-minute steps.

ThemeSystem, Light or Dark. System follows your desktop's setting.

Data

The Data section shows where your database is stored and when the last backup was made. It has these actions:

  • Export JSON: save all your data as a readable JSON file.
  • Import JSON: replace your data with an exported file. A backup is made first.
  • Back up now: make a backup immediately.
  • Restore a backup: choose an automatic backup to restore. Your current data is backed up first, and the app reloads.
  • Open data folder: show the folder containing your database and backups.

See Data and backups for details.
