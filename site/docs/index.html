Getting started

Timeblock helps you plan your day in time blocks, record what actually happened, and compare both against weekly goals. This page covers installation and a first week with the app.

Core ideas

Timeblock is built around three ideas:

  • Plan and Actual are separate. Every day has a Plan lane for what you intend to do and an Actual lane for what really happened.
  • Only confirmed time counts. A planned block is not counted as actual time until you confirm it. Your weekly numbers never assume the plan went as expected.
  • Goals have weekly targets. Each goal can require at least, or at most, a number of hours per week. The review compares target, plan and actual for every goal.

Install

Download the file for your system from the download page.

SystemFileNotes
Linux.rpm, .deb, .AppImageThe AppImage needs glibc 2.39 or newer (Fedora 40+, Ubuntu 24.04+).
macOS.dmgUniversal build for Apple silicon and Intel.
Windows-setup.exe, .msi64-bit.

Builds are not code-signed yet. macOS blocks the first launch: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On Windows, if SmartScreen warns about an unrecognized app, click More info → Run anyway.

Only one copy of Timeblock runs at a time. Launching it again brings the open window to the front.

The four views

The sidebar on the left switches between the four main views. Each has a single-key shortcut.

ViewKeyPurpose
DaydPlan and Actual lanes for one day. Where you spend most of your time.
WeekwAll seven days. Past days show Plan and Actual side by side.
GoalsgGoals, weekly targets and outcome checklists.
ReviewrTarget, plan and actual for every goal, with a reflection note.

Use t to jump to today, and [ and ] to move to the previous or next day or week.

Your first week

  1. Create your goals. Open Goals (g), click New goal, and give it a name, a color and an optional weekly target. See Goals and reviews.
  2. Plan your days. In the Day or Week view, drag in the Plan lane to create blocks, and assign each block a goal. See Planning your day.
  3. Record what happened. As the day goes on, confirm planned blocks that happened (a), skip those that didn't (s), and add anything unplanned directly to the Actual lane.
  4. Review the week. Open Review (r) to compare your targets with the time you planned and spent, tick off outcomes and write a short reflection.
