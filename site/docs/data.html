Data and backups

Timeblock keeps all of your data in a single SQLite file on your computer. No account is needed. If you sign in to an optional sync account, your plan is also kept in step between your computers.

Where your data is stored

SystemFolder
Linux~/.local/share/com.shreysingh.timeblock/
macOS~/Library/Application Support/com.shreysingh.timeblock/
Windows%APPDATA%\com.shreysingh.timeblock\

The folder contains:

  • data.sqlite3: everything (blocks, goals, targets, outcomes, notes and settings).
  • backups/: automatic backups.

To open the folder, use Settings → Open data folder.

Automatic backups

Timeblock copies your database to backups/:

  • at most once a day,
  • before every import,
  • before upgrading the database to a new version of the app.

The newest 14 backups are kept. If an automatic backup fails, the app tells you.

Restoring a backup

Use Settings → Restore a backup and pick a backup from the list. Your current data is backed up first, so a restore can be undone by restoring again.

To restore by hand, quit Timeblock and copy a file from backups/ over data.sqlite3.

Export and import

Settings → Export JSON writes all of your data to a readable JSON file. Keep it as an extra backup, or move your data to another computer with Settings → Import JSON.

Importing replaces all current data. Timeblock makes a backup before importing, so you can restore it if needed.

How saving works

Every change is saved as you make it. If a save fails, Timeblock reloads your data from disk, discards changes that were waiting behind the failed save, and tells you. Undo history is cleared in that case. When you close the window, the app waits up to three seconds for pending saves to finish.

Sync (optional)

In Settings → Sync & account, sign in or create an account to sync your plan between your computers. Changes sync within a few seconds; the cloud icon in the top bar shows the status, and clicking it syncs right away. Everything keeps working offline and catches up when you're back online.

The first time you sign in on a device that already has data, you choose whether to merge it with the account, use the account's data, or upload this device's data. The desktop app makes a backup first. Signing out keeps your data on the computer.

Device settings such as the theme and notifications are not synced. The sync server is self-hosted; its source is in server/ in the repository.

Privacy

Timeblock has no telemetry and needs no account. Without a sync account, your plan never leaves your computer. The app only goes online to check for updates and to download calendar subscriptions you add, and, if you sign in, to sync with your account's server.
